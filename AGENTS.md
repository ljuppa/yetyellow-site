## Writing voice

The author is a European engineering leader living in the Netherlands and is not a native English speaker. Write clear international English in a Dutch professional register: direct, sober and precise. Use British spelling. Prefer short, straightforward sentence structures and familiar words.

Do not imitate American corporate or marketing language. Avoid hype, idioms, forced conversational warmth, rhetorical flourish and unearned self-promotion.

For every text or description, write from concrete work, judgement and constraints. Make claims specific or remove them. If a sentence could fit another AI advisory website, rewrite or delete it.
